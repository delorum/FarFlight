## Final prompt

Use case: lighting-weather.
Asset type: 16:9 widescreen background artwork for a game's main menu and pause menu.
Input image 1: edit target and composition/style reference, the existing daytime Far Flight title artwork.
Primary request: Create a matching night-time version of exactly this scene. The same rugged cream-and-amber cargo biplane, in the same rear three-quarter view, is approaching to land at the same small remote desert airport beneath the low cloud layer. Keep the aircraft in the right half at the same scale and placement; preserve its silhouette, wing/strut anatomy, fixed landing gear, portholes and paint pattern. Keep the same runway, hangars, small buildings, desert rocks, distant mesas and clouds in the same composition and perspective.
Lighting/mood: unambiguously deep night, not sunset or dusk. Dark blue-green charcoal sky, cloud and fog, dim moonlit highlights on the plane, night-dark desert and airport. Warm amber light from hangar doors and windows, believable runway edge lights and threshold lights, small navigation lights and a subtle landing light on the aircraft. The aircraft and airport remain readable in the darkness without looking daytime-bright.
Style/medium: preserve the original detailed atmospheric painterly gouache/etched aviation concept-art look and fine grain; no photorealistic restyle.
Composition/framing: Preserve the wide 16:9 framing. The left third must remain quiet dark fog/sky with minimal detail and NO bright lights, suitable for a beige title and vertical menu overlay. Preserve the approaching plane and the airport on the right.
Constraints: change only time of day, lighting and associated night lights. No new aircraft or people, no new airport structures, no UI, no typography, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Opaque background, not transparent. Make a separate sibling asset; do not alter the reference file.
